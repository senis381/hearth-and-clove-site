04 Our Story

Maren Voss opened the doors in 2019

Nothing about the place is trying to be more than what it is.

Maren trained in Lyon for three years before she came home to Copenhagen — long shifts at a small boulangerie where the baker refused to explain anything twice and the bread was better for it. She left knowing how to laminate dough at 4am with her eyes half closed, and how little that actually has to do with running a bakery.

Hearth & Clove started as one oven, one espresso machine, and eleven tables in a space that used to be a bicycle repair shop on Blomstergade. The first winter, Maren baked alone before sunrise and served the counter herself until the regulars started showing up early enough to help carry trays. Some of them still do.

The name is plain on purpose — a hearth for the ovens, clove for the spice drawer she never stops reaching for. Nothing about the place is trying to be more than what it is: bread that takes the time it takes, coffee pulled by someone paying attention, and enough chairs that nobody has to rush their cup.

— Maren

05 What we believe

Our Philosophy

Slow fermentation, always

Our doughs rest overnight, sometimes two nights. Good bread can't be rushed, and we're not interested in the shortcut that pretends it can.

Local when it makes sense

Danish rye, Danish butter, Danish eggs. The coffee travels a long way to get here — nearly everything else doesn't have to.

A seat, not just a bag

Half our tables face the window on purpose. This is a place to stay a while, not just a counter to grab from on the way past.

Honest pricing

One price on the board. No upsell script at the till, no surprise at the register — what you see is what you pay.

07 Take your time

A place to slow down

There's no rush here, and no Wi-Fi password taped to the espresso machine to hint otherwise. The window seats fill first, but the back tables are quieter, and the light in the afternoon comes in low and warm through the east-facing glass.

Bring a book, bring a friend, bring nothing at all. The chairs don't have a time limit on them.
